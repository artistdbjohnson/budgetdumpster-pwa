Experience Counts

Budget Dumpster is a national dumpster rental company that provides local, personalized service. We've been in business since 2009 and have rented out more than 1 million dumpsters.

In 2015, we started our dumpster donation program as a way to give back to local communities. We’ve partnered with Habitat for Humanity, ReStore, Rebuilding Together and many other organizations.
